Home mining for Bitcoin Cash II

Mine BCH2 at home.

Forge Solo is one free app for mining Bitcoin Cash II (BCH2): your own BCH2 node, a place for your miners to connect, and a dashboard that shows how it’s going.

Get Forge Solo New to mining? Start here
  • Free and open source (MIT)
  • No pool fee
  • Umbrel, Windows and Linux

Before you start

What you need

Three things. You don’t need to know how mining works yet.

  1. A miner

    A small one like a Bitaxe, which sits on a desk, or a bigger ASIC miner. You can connect several.

    Connects on port 3333

  2. A computer that stays on

    An Umbrel box, a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Linux machine, even a Raspberry Pi. Forge Solo mines while it runs.

    Pays rewards to

  3. Your BCH2 address

    Where your rewards go. You enter it once, in Forge Solo’s Settings. Blocks pay it directly, with no pool wallet in between.

The idea

How it works

Mining is a race to find the next BCH2 block: your miner makes guess after guess, very fast. A winning guess makes the new block, and the block’s reward is paid from inside that block.

Your own BCH2 node, which keeps a full copy of the blockchain, builds every block your miners work on. In Settings, under How blocks pay, you choose Solo or TIDES pool.

On your own

Solo

A block you find pays your BCH2 address in full, straight from the block itself.

Who mines Your miners
Which blocks pay A block you find
Who gets paid You, all of it
When you’re paid
Nothing until you find a block, then all of it.
Fee
No pool fee.
Also mines 1175 (ESF)
Yes, a second chain, at no extra hashrate cost: on Umbrel and Windows, once you set a 1175 payout address.

As a team

TIDES

Team up with other miners through Forge Pool (pool.bch2.org): your work goes into its TIDES window, where Forge Pool registers the work of everyone mining with it. While Forge Pool can’t be reached, Forge Solo mines solo.

Who mines You and others in the window
Which blocks pay Every TIDES block
Who gets paid your work all work in the window Everyone in the window, by their work
When you’re paid
At every TIDES block (a block found on work Forge Pool registered), by your share of the work in the window. An amount under 546 satoshis, a tiny fraction of one BCH2, is carried to a later one.
Fee
0.00%, and no pool wallet.
Also mines 1175 (ESF)
No, 1175 merge-mining is off in TIDES mode.

Either way there is no payout schedule: the block pays you directly, and its reward can be spent after 100 blocks. See the live TIDES window

Get Forge Solo

Pick the computer it will live on. Windows and Linux downloads come only from Forge Solo’s releases on GitHub. Umbrel installs it from an app store.

  • Umbrel

    BCH2 + 1175 merge-mining

    In Umbrel, under Settings › App Store › Community App Stores › Add, paste:

    https://github.com/BitcoincashII/umbrel-app-store

    Then open BCH2 Community Apps and install Forge Solo.

    Umbrel directions

  • Windows

    BCH2 + 1175 merge-mining

    64-bit Windows 10 or 11 on an x64 PC, or Windows 11 on ARM. On the latest release, under Assets, download:

    ForgeSolo-Setup-<version>.exe

    The first time, Windows shows two prompts, explained below.

    Download for Windows from GitHub Windows directions

  • Linux

    BCH2 only

    Fully static: it runs on any distribution with kernel 3.17 or newer. On the latest release, under Assets, download:

    forge-solo-<version>-linux-<arch>.tar.gz

    For <arch>, use what uname -m prints: x86_64 or i686 on PCs; aarch64, armv7l or armv6l on ARM boards such as the Raspberry Pi; riscv64 on RISC‑V boards.

    Download for Linux from GitHub Linux directions

On Windows, expect two prompts.

Forge Solo is not yet signed by a certificate Windows trusts.

  1. SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Windows asks whether Windows Command Processor may make changes to your device: choose Yes, so miners on your network can reach Forge Solo.

If Smart App Control blocks it, the Windows directions say what to do.

After you install

Then, connect your miner

  1. Set your payout address

    Open Forge Solo’s dashboard and go to its Settings page. Enter your BCH2 address and save; saving asks for Forge Solo’s password. On Umbrel and Windows, add a 1175 payout address too, to merge-mine in solo mode.

  2. Point your miner at Forge Solo

    In your miner’s settings, enter this as its stratum address, the server it sends its work to:

    stratum+tcp://<your machine’s address>:3333

    The dashboard shows the address. The worker name is only a label: rig1, bitaxe, anything.

Where to find the dashboard and the password

Umbrel
Open the Forge Solo app; umbrelOS shows the password when you open it.
Windows
The dashboard opens when Forge Solo starts. Password: right-click the Forge Solo icon in the notification area, then Copy Settings Password.
Linux
Open http://127.0.0.1:3080 on that machine. Password: DASHBOARD_PASSWORD in secrets.env, in the data directory.

On Windows, set your network to Private.

While it is Public, miners on your network cannot connect. In Windows Settings, under Network & internet, open your connection’s properties and set its network profile to Private. Windows 11 makes new networks Public.

Renting hashpower? Rentals from NiceHash and MiningRigRentals use port 3335. They connect from the internet, so they reach you only once you forward that port in your router.

Everything else is in the README: connecting a miner, the ports to open in your router and rented hashpower.
